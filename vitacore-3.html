General tips

  • Sit where motion is felt least — front seat of a car, over the wing on a plane, mid-ship on a boat.
  • Look toward the horizon or a fixed distant point; avoid reading or screens.
  • Get fresh air and avoid strong odors when possible.
  • Keep your head as still as you can relative to the vehicle.
  • Avoid heavy or greasy meals right before traveling; ginger is a commonly used natural option.
  • Ask a pharmacist about over-the-counter options suited to you before you travel.

Gentle reminders

  • Aim for variety across the week rather than perfection in any one meal.
  • Eating regularly through the day tends to feel better than skipping meals.
  • Slowing down and noticing hunger and fullness cues is worth more than any single food choice.
  • There's room for treats — the goal is a sustainable pattern, not a strict rulebook.
  • If eating feels stressful or hard to manage, a doctor or registered dietitian can help far more than an app.
